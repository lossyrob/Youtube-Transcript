# The IDE Died: Vibe Coding is Reshaping Technology
**Speaker**: Steve Yegge & Gene Kim (Authors, "Vibe Coding" Book)
**Day**: 1 | **Talk #**: 05

---

## 🎯 Quick Read (TL;DR)

Steve Yegge and Gene Kim—both legends in developer productivity circles—presented their thesis from their upcoming "Vibe Coding" book: AI is reshaping coding and organizations at a scale potentially **100x larger than agile/DevOps/cloud/CI-CD combined**. Gene shared case studies showing leaders building production apps in days that teams quoted months for, support teams doubling their shipping velocity, and Cisco requiring 100 top leaders to vibe-code a feature into production. The central insight: trust in AI tools grows linearly with usage time—everyone who dismisses AI coding after an hour is making conclusions on incomplete data. The talk argues we may be the last generation to code by hand, and organizations that don't adapt will be reshaped by those who do.

**Core Thesis**: Vibe coding—iterative conversation resulting in AI writing your code—is not just faster but fundamentally reshapes what's possible, who can ship code, and how technology organizations are structured.

**Top 3 Takeaways**:
1. Trust in AI coding tools grows linearly with usage time—those who dismiss it after trying briefly haven't invested enough hours
2. Engineers should challenge themselves to spend as much on tokens per day as their salary (~$500-$1000/day) to maximize cognitive leverage
3. Case studies show leaders building in 5 days what teams quoted 5 months for, fundamentally changing power dynamics in orgs

## 🔮 Implications & Predictions

**For Developers**: 
Invest significant hours in AI coding tools—the research shows trust and competence grow linearly with time. Challenge yourself to spend substantially on tokens daily. Your value proposition is shifting from typing code to directing AI effectively.

**For AI Tool Builders**: 
The coordination cost reduction is a massive opportunity. Tools that help teams manage high-velocity AI-generated code (merge conflicts, review processes) are needed. The "one engineer per repo" problem is unsolved.

**For Engineering Leaders**: 
Consider requiring leaders to vibe-code features into production (like Cisco). The power dynamics are shifting—leaders can now bypass their own teams for certain work. Junior engineers who embrace AI may outpace seniors who don't.

**Contrarian/Surprising Takes**: 
- The book authors advocate spending hundreds of dollars per day on tokens—framing it as "mechanical advantage" rather than cost
- The most junior engineer maintaining a leader's vibe-coded app is "out-earning everyone"—inverts traditional seniority value
- The suggestion that we should hire based on AI coding ability rather than traditional coding skills
- "Support ships code" becoming a serious strategy (connects to Lisa Orr's Zapier talk)
